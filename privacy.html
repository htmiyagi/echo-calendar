Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers two free student projects that share one Google sign-in: Echo Calendar (this website) and FIVES (a desktop app for macOS). Both let you add events to your Google Calendar by typing plain sentences.

What we access

When you connect your Google account, the app asks for one permission: to see, add and edit events on your Google Calendar (the calendar.events scope). It uses this only to show your upcoming events and to create, change or delete the events you approve. It never acts on your calendar without you asking.

Where your data goes

  • Only between you and Google. There is no server of ours in the middle. Your events go straight from Google to the app on your device.
  • Echo Calendar runs entirely in your browser. Your sign-in lasts only while the page is open.
  • FIVES saves a sign-in key in a file on your own Mac, readable only by your user account, so you don't have to sign in again each time. It never leaves your computer.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your calendar data or personal information. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How to disconnect

In FIVES, press DISCONNECT in Settings (this also deletes the saved key). For either app, you can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
